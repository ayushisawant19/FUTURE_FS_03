### 1. Who the Business Is

- Romantic anniversaries, proposals, and date nights

- Baby shower hampers and newborn blessings

Riwayat began with an intimate passion for thoughtful, heartfelt gifting—aiming to make every celebration special, personal, and unforgettable. Unlike generic e-commerce boxes, Riwayat’s signature hallmark is its **handcrafted luxury packaging**: tactile velvet and rigid keepsake trunks, coordinated silk and satin ribbons, real wax seal stamps, and hand-inscribed deckle-edged calligraphy cards.

### 2. What Problem the Website Solves
Previously, Riwayat operated exclusively through its Instagram business page. While Instagram provided visual interest, it created critical business bottlenecks:
1. **DM Fatigue & Repetitive Queries:** The owner spent hours answering the same questions about box styles, ribbon colors, and pricing.
2. **Order Chaos & Lack of Structure:** Custom orders without a guided framework led to vague briefs, missed delivery details, and last-minute panic.
3. **Fuzzy Delivery Policies:** Customers did not understand that bespoke curation requires advance notice (minimum 2 days, ideally 7+ days) and that delivery charges across Pune are distance-quoted.
4. **Trust & Payment Hesitation:** Asking customers to transfer money via random UPI IDs in DMs created drop-offs for high-ticket wedding and corporate orders.

**How the Website Solves This:**
- **Guided "Build Your Own Hamper" Multi-Step Builder:** Lets customers select recipient, occasion, budget bracket, packaging theme, items (chocolates, florals, candles, keepsakes), and personalized calligraphy text step-by-step.
- **Interactive Gift-Wrapping Visualizer:** Customers can test box finishes (Royal Burgundy Velvet, Warm Ivory Matte, Espresso Chest, Wooden Trunk) and silk ribbon palettes with real-time visual feedback.
- **Clear Studio Notice Rules:** Enforces a minimum 2-day notice, blocks invalid dates with a friendly Instagram/WhatsApp redirect, and establishes distance-based Pune delivery transparency.
- **Structured Enquiry & Payment Pipeline:** Replaces DM ambiguity with clear status tracking (New → Quote Sent → Confirmed → Preparing → Delivered) backed by secure payment link integration (Razorpay).

### 3. How It Helps Attract Customers
- **Local Pune SEO & Discovery:** Built with LocalBusiness JSON-LD structured schema targeting Koregaon Park, Baner, Kothrud, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, and Hinjawadi for high-intent search queries ("luxury gift hampers Pune", "customized birthday boxes Pune").
- **Seamless Instagram-to-Web Bridging:** Strategic dual CTAs (`ig.me/m/riwayat_placeholder` & WhatsApp click-to-chat) allow social media visitors to transition effortlessly between chat and structured ordering.
- **Interactive 60-Second Gift Finder Quiz:** Engages undecided visitors and immediately converts them into qualified enquiries by suggesting the ideal hamper match.
- **Social Proof & Visual Trust:** Verified testimonials with local Pune locations and an integrated Instagram order gallery showcase real past deliveries.
- **Seasonal Festival Urgency:** Dynamic festive countdown timers (e.g. Diwali & Wedding season slots) drive prompt bookings before atelier capacity closes.
- **Referral & Loyalty Program:** Encourages word-of-mouth recommendations among Pune families and corporate HR teams.

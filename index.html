Our Story

A Passion for Thoughtful, Personalized Gifting

Riwayat was born in Pune out of a simple observation: in an era of impersonal retail boxes and hurried logistics, real heartfelt gifting had lost its magic. We set out to build an atelier where every gift tells a story—wrapped with intention, beauty, and warmth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom hampers, advance booking, and Pune deliveries.

We deliver across all prominent areas of Pune and PCMC—including Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Baner, Aundh, Kothrud, Senapati Bapat Road, Magarpatta, Hadapsar, Wakad, and Hinjawadi. Delivery charges are calculated by actual travel distance from our Pune atelier via our personal delivery team and included directly in your bespoke quote.
We recommend booking at least 7 days in advance for birthdays, anniversaries, and personal milestones to guarantee full personalization. For urgent orders, a minimum notice of 2 days is required subject to atelier slot availability. Last-minute modifications (less than 48 hours) are subject to availability and incur a nominal restructuring fee.
Because every Riwayat hamper is bespoke! Prices vary based on your choice of gourmet treats, luxury keepsakes, fresh floral additions, packaging styles, and personalized items. Once you submit an enquiry, our styling team shares an exact itemized quote within 2–4 hours.
Once we share your custom quote and you confirm the details, we generate a secure Razorpay payment link. You can pay via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit cards, or Netbanking. Orders are confirmed immediately upon payment receipt.
Because each hamper is custom-curated and perishable items or personalized keepsakes cannot be reused, we do not accept general cancellations or returns once confirmed. Studio Policies & Delivery Terms

1. Advance Notice & Urgent Orders

We recommend booking at least 7 days before your celebration. Urgent orders require a minimum of 2 days' notice and are subject to slot availability. Dates less than 48 hours away cannot be booked online and require direct WhatsApp confirmation.

2. Pune Delivery & Distance Charges

We deliver across Pune & PCMC within scheduled 3–4 hour windows. Delivery charges are paid by the customer and calculated by distance from our atelier.

3. Cancellation & Refunds

Because all hampers are customized and contain artisanal perishables or personalized keepsakes, no refunds are provided for general cancellations or change of mind. In case of transit damage, photos must be provided within 24 hours of delivery for immediate replacement or refund.

4. Order Modifications

Minor adjustments can be accommodated if requested at least 48 hours before delivery. Last-minute modifications incur additional restructuring fees.
